Welcome

Your Mediterranean home in Lopar.

The Lazarić family has been welcoming guests for years to their house in San Marino, a settlement in Lopar on the northwestern side of the island of Rab.

Discover Lopar & Rab

Lopar — 22 sandy beaches.

Lopar lies on a peninsula in the northwestern part of the island of Rab. It is best known for Paradise Beach — 1.5 km of fine sand surrounded by pine forest. The town also features many smaller sandy beaches, a sports centre, restaurants and traditional taverns.

For those looking for something different, Lopar offers three nudist beaches: Sahara, Ciganka, and Stolac. According to legend, this is the birthplace of St. Marin, a 4th-century stonemason and founder of the Republic of San Marino.

Rab Island — pearl of Kvarner.

Rab Island is located in the northern Adriatic and is part of the Kvarner archipelago. Locals have been engaged in organized tourism for more than 110 years, and the island is known for its mild Mediterranean climate which, according to scientific research, has a positive effect on heart and respiratory conditions.

The town of Rab attracts visitors with its medieval old town and distinctive bell towers. The island is accessible via the Jablanac–Mišnjak and Valbiska–Lopar ferry lines, and is about 110 km from Rijeka and 190 km from Zagreb.
